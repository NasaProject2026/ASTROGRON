How ASTRAGRON Works: Plain Language & Scientific Foundations

ASTRAGRON connects macro-scale Earth observations with local farm research questions while enforcing strict scientific truth boundaries. Here is how satellite observations, local evidence, and multi-scenario evaluations fit together.

01 · PLAIN-LANGUAGE EXPLANATION FOR FARMERS & PLANNERS

Why satellite data alone cannot tell you what crop to plant tomorrow

Earth-observing satellites (such as NASA POWER, GPM IMERG, SMAP, and Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2) provide valuable regional context: historical rainfall patterns, temperature swings, regional soil moisture trends, and canopy greenness. However, a satellite pixel covering 10 to 50 kilometers cannot see whether your specific north field has a working irrigation pipe, how much plant-available nitrate is in your subsoil, or what your legal water extraction permit allows.

Instead of pretending to be an oracle that outputs a single “best farm plan,” ASTRAGRON tests a finite set of candidate 5-year crop rotations against historical dry, median, and wet weather analogues—and clearly reports when zero plans meet every water-service requirement.

03 · M14 DECISION CONTRACT & DATASET MODE SEPARATION

Synthetic UI Demonstration vs. Recorded M14 Corpus vs. Live Scientific Backend

To guarantee zero scientific misrepresentation, ASTRAGRON separates three operating modes: (1) SYNTHETIC_UI_DEMO (active default, evaluating 12 synthetic policies × 9 scenario states = 108 cases across 3 fields totaling 24.0 ha: F1=6.0 ha non-irrigable, F2=8.0 ha non-irrigable, F3=10.0 ha irrigable); (2) RECORDED_M14_RESEARCH (SHA-256 verified read-only M14 corpus spanning 16 recorded policies × 9 states = 144 cases for Uppsala SE_UPPSALA plus M4 source-coverage records, failing closed with SOURCE_NOT_PROVIDED whenever integrity verification fails); and (3) REAL_SCIENTIFIC_BACKEND (which fails closed with BACKEND_NOT_CONNECTED when the protected scientific backend is offline).

Separation of Nitrogen & Frozen Economic Cases

Nitrogen: Mass-conserving accounting only; zero field-qualified N calibration exists and N-enabled crop ranking is not established.

Frozen Financial Reference (Kept Strictly Separate): A separate frozen assumption-based economic study modeled a five-year operating surplus of €21,005.20 and a price-stress outcome of −€20,779.12. Those financial figures are never mixed into NASA-driven water scenario results, never presented as realized farm profits, and ET deficits are never used to infer crop yield or financial return.
