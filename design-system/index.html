Phase 1 Creative Exploration: Three Evaluated Directions

VARIATION A · NADIR TELEMETRY HUD

Full-bleed top-down satellite grid with dense HUD corner brackets. Rejected because dense sci-fi chrome competed with serious numeric legibility on mobile screens.

VARIATION B (SELECTED) · ASYMMETRIC ORBITAL LIMB + CALM MISSION CONTROL

Deep space (#050A12) on the left 42% for pristine editorial typography, photorealistic Earth limb + EO satellite on the right 58%, transitioning into disciplined deep-navy (#07111F / #0D2031) utilitarian research panels. Best balance of emotional awe and clarity.

VARIATION C · SPLIT GLOBE WORKBENCH

Immediate 50/50 split screen on `/`. Rejected for the landing route because first-time visitors needed a 5-second human proposition before entering the geospatial workbench.
