SCIENTIFIC & DECISION GLOSSARY

ET0 / ETc / ETa (Evapotranspiration)

ET0 is reference atmospheric evaporative demand (mm/day); ETc is crop-stage water demand under non-stressed conditions; ETa is actual modeled evapotranspiration given available root-zone moisture.

Minimax Regret (MINIMAX_REGRET)

Ranks plans by their largest disadvantage versus the best comparable plan in each tested scenario. In the synthetic UI demonstration, Policy SYNTH-POL-02 has 640.25 m³ equivalent maximum regret.

Worst-Case Modeled ET Shortfall (MINIMIZE_WORST_ET_SHORTFALL)

Ranks comparable plans by their highest aggregate evapotranspiration deficit across all tested scenario states. In the synthetic UI demonstration, Policy SYNTH-POL-01 has 38,420.50 m³ equivalent worst-state shortfall.

Pareto Frontier (PARETO_FRONTIER / NO_UNIQUE_WINNER)

The subset of comparable plans where no other candidate is simultaneously better on every criterion (shortfall, regret, and mean water withdrawal). Returns NO_UNIQUE_WINNER by design.

Calendar-Invalid Policy Exclusion

If a policy schedules a sowing window before the preceding crop’s harvest window finishes (e.g., Policy SYNTH-POL-12 on Field F3 Year 3), it is excluded from comparable numeric ranking and its shortfall/regret remain NULL.

WGS84 (EPSG:4326) Coordinate Order

The M4 evidence contract specifies coordinates strictly as [longitude, latitude] in decimal degrees.

UNDERSTANDING ABSTENTION & FAIL-CLOSED STATES
Why does the Decision Overview say “FIELD_RECOMMENDATION_ELIGIBILITY: BLOCKED”?In the Uppsala synthetic demonstration (3 fields, 24.0 ha), non-irrigable Field F1 (6.0 ha) and Field F2 (8.0 ha) experience deficits exceeding the research-assumed 100.0 mm service threshold during the 2018 dry weather analogue. Across 16 tested policies × 9 scenarios (144 matrix cases, 4 calendar-invalid), 0 of 12 comparable policies pass all 9 states.
Why do Kenya (Nyeri) and Australia (Wagga Wagga) return “MODEL_UNQUALIFIED” on Decision, Plan, and Climate pages?While NASA satellites observe rainfall and surface reflectance globally, crop phenology and water-service models must be calibrated for local agro-ecologies. ASTRAGRON enforces strict cross-site isolation and never leaks Swedish crops, climate series, or policy tables onto non-Uppsala sites.
PRIVACY, GEOLOCATION & LOCAL DEMO STORAGE

• No Covert Cloud Upload: Farm polygons, coordinates, and wizard inputs are stored strictly in your browser’s localStorage and can be wiped at any time on the Settings page.

• Consent-First Geolocation: The browser geolocation API is never invoked automatically—only when you explicitly click “Use My Location” on the Earth Explorer.

• No NASA Endorsement: ASTRAGRON references public NASA Earth-observation products with proper source attribution and makes zero claim of NASA endorsement or certification.
